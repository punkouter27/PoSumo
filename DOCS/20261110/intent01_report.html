ELO

Self-play rating against its own past selves. The accept/reject signal for a fight run: judge the SHAPE, not a number. Resets to 1200 at the start of every run, so it is not comparable to earlier runs.

Round length (s)

Mean seconds until a round is decided. The training referee draws at 20 s, so lower means more decisive bouts.

Mean episode reward

Shaping plus the ±1 result. Includes the new edge-brace term. NOT the acceptance criterion: reward can rise while ELO falls.

Value loss

The critic started from scratch (an ONNX holds no critic). This should fall as it catches up with a policy that is already trained.
